Social Progression, Jobs & Living Economy

The Canonical Career Tree

Working is not a pause between missions: the work IS the game. Organized by autonomous profession trees: explore the 10 ranks of Lunar Industrial Sensors, 7 positions of the Metropolitan Pharmacy Chain, 7 culinary ranks of the Restaurant Sector, Autonomous Gigs, and Supermarket Retail. Each branch features distinct visual sequences, promotion choices, degree requirements, and lifetime career modifiers.

Hidden Mechanics

Invisible Performance

There is no immersion-breaking progress bar on screen. Worker evaluation in SPRF is dynamic and human: punctuality, behavior, and coworker relationships are quietly observed and directly influence your professional standing and promotion.

Rigor & Discipline

Severe Error Penalties

Mistakes aren't shrugged off in SPRF. On demanding difficulty levels, mistakes and blunders are punished with severe score deductions that can wipe out days of honest effort.

Special Perks

Permanent Career Perks

Advancing through rigorous roles or graduating with specialized qualifications unlocks lasting perks and permanent salary advantages across related career sectors.
