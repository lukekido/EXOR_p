Penal Code & Punitive Physics · SPRF

Crime, Punishment & Real Consequences

In EXORDIUM there are no arcade police that forget your hit-and-runs after you respray a car. The judicial system is unforgiving: pedestrian collisions with realistic ragdoll physics, fines multiplied severely if you flee the scene, police workplace searches, and the imposing Prison Cube at the southern edge of town.

Legal Escalation System

Real-Time Criminal Escalation

The three legal stages triggered after a vehicular collision or pedestrian impact:

PHASE 1 · 10 TO 30 SECONDS

[Potential Suspect]

Triggered immediately upon crash impact. If the victim survives and you remain at the scene awaiting emergency response, you are held liable only for civil property damages and insurance claims.

PHASE 2 · FIRST 2 DAYS

[Fleeing Suspect]

Triggered instantly if you move more than 20 meters away from the victim before medical response arrives! Spending more than 4 continuous seconds in a police cruiser's cone of vision results in immediate arrest with no bail!

PHASE 3 · AFTER 2 DAYS

[Wanted Fugitive]

A judicial search warrant is issued. Police officers will raid your workplace: if you work in an office, officers search commercial buildings during business hours. Ride-hailing apps are blocked!

Ballistic Simulation & Body Dynamics

Visceral Impact & Ragdoll Physics

I developed pedestrian impact physics to feel authentically visceral: "Pedestrian collisions, hood throws, and secondary pavement impacts deliver the exact weight and tactile punch I always aimed for in this game!"

Collision Speed Body Dynamics (Ragdoll) Legal Consequence
10 to 20 km/h Pedestrian falls to their knees, curses at the driver, stands up, and limps away. Civil Fine only.
20 to 40 km/h Bone fracture sound, body slides onto the hood, emergency ambulance called. Aggravated Injury.
40 to 60 km/h Body is launched over the hood and rolls across the roof. Stretcher extraction. Driver's License Revocation.
Above 60 km/h High mortality collision. Blood spatters across windshield and remains for 3 in-game days! Manslaughter & Penitentiary.

⚠️ Secondary Post-Impact Collisions

If the launched body strikes a street lamp, utility pole, or brick wall at speeds above 30 km/h, the physical trauma automatically escalates a severity tier!

Penitentiary System of SPRF

The Prison Cube

Playable Incarceration Routine

Situated at the southern edge of the map, past the simple homes of the outskirts, stands the formidable Prison Cube: a monolithic concrete fortress measuring 56 meters per side and 36 meters tall, surrounded by 5-meter razor walls and spotlights.

Cell Routine Living Incarceration

Waking up in a 6x3m cell with an iron bunk, barred window, prison courtyard exercise yard, and scheduled meal shifts.

Consequence in the World Time Never Stops

While you are incarcerated, the 24-week campaign clock keeps ticking relentlessly toward the point of no return!

Life After Release Zero Credit & Back to Start

Serving your sentence releases you back to the street with an empty bank balance. You must rebuild your career from the bottom rung!
